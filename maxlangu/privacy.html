2. Principle

Maxlangu is a language-learning app that deliberately works without a user account and without collecting personal data. You can use the app without registering or providing any personal details.

3. Data that stays on your device

Your progress (words learned, points, level, streak, settings) is stored only on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties. Deleting the app removes it.

4. Purchases

The full version is bought through the Apple App Store. Payment and management of the purchase are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details (such as card numbers) and store none. Apple's privacy policy applies in addition.

5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are created as local notifications directly on your device. No data is sent to us.

6. Text-to-speech

To read words aloud, the app uses your device's own speech function (Apple). No personal data is processed by us in doing so.

7. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

Maxlangu contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and shares no data with third parties for advertising purposes.

8. Children

The app is aimed at a general audience and knowingly collects no data from children.

9. Your rights

As we collect no personal data, we hold none. If you have questions about privacy you can reach us any time at lukasbeutler@gmx.de.

10. Changes

We may adjust this policy when the app changes. The current version is always on this page.
