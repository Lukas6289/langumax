Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

After installing, you have full access to every feature for five days. After that the daily flashcards (Daily Words) stay free; everything else is unlocked with a single purchase. No subscription.

I bought the full version but it is gone (new device)

Open the app, tap "Unlock Pro" and then "Restore purchase". Important: sign in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Which direction can I learn in?

Both. On first launch you pick your native language and the language you want to learn. The app then asks in the matching direction.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Your progress is stored only on your device. We collect no personal data. Details are in our privacy policy.

How do I delete my progress?

Uninstall the app – that removes everything stored locally.

What is the difference to Langumax?

Maxlangu teaches the same words and games, but starts from English. Langumax is the German edition.
